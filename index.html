TUNAROO

Your channels, in your order.

Tunaroo is a TV player for Android TV boxes. It opens on your own lists of favourite channels, in the order you set, with a guide that follows the same order.

What it does

  • Your own lists. Create lists such as Sport or Everyday and put the channels in any order.
  • No double-ups. HD, FHD and backup copies of a channel appear once.
  • Picks a working copy. If one copy of a channel fails, it moves to the next without asking.
  • Guide. Now and next on every row, and a three-hour grid for each list.
  • Remote only. Everything works from the direction pad, OK and Back.

What you need

Tunaroo is a player only. It contains no channels and no content. You sign in with the details from your own TV provider: a provider login (Xtream) or an M3U playlist link, plus an optional guide link.

Install

  1. Download the file to the box, or copy it across on a USB stick.
  2. Open the file. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from that app. Allow it, then choose Install.
  3. Open Tunaroo, enter your provider details, and add channels to your first list.
Tunaroo is a personal project. It does not provide, host or sell any channels or streams.
