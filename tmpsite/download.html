Early test builds. Tunaroo has been checked on test devices and is now being tried on real TV boxes and Macs. If something doesn't work, tell us and it'll get fixed in the next build.

Not on the list yet

There's no version for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Windows or Intel Macs yet. The Fire TV Stick 4K Select and the 2026 Fire TV Stick HD can't install apps from outside the Amazon Appstore, so Tunaroo can't run on them.

Updating

On Android TV boxes and Fire TV Sticks, Tunaroo checks for a new build once a day and offers Update now, keeping your provider details and lists. On a Mac, download the new build from this page and replace the old one. You can check which version you have in Settings, at the bottom of the list.
